02 / THE MODEL

A small input.
A documented chain of calculations.

One observed event date per year, expressed as day of year (DOY). Each run derives the forecast year from the last observation.

01

Read the observations

Load consecutive Year,DOY records. January 1 is day 1. Input checks catch missing years, duplicates and invalid values.

02

Find the dominant period

Transform the observed series, identify the strongest positive-frequency spectral peak, and use its period to select the smoothing window.

03

Calculate the means

Form two-year means, then apply the selected rolling window. Early averages expand until enough history is available.

04

Forecast the next year

Produce a quadratic estimate and an OFBM estimate, with calendar dates, plots and explicit method notes.

READ THE LABELS CAREFULLY

The historical “FFT forecast” label refers to a quadratic forecast after FFT-selected smoothing. OFBM preserves the original filter’s coefficient updates. The two-year preprocessing step follows the workbooks; it is not explicitly prescribed in the paper.
